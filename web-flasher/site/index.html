Flash your Mi A3.

Xiaomi Mi A3 (laurel_sprout) only. Writes the generic ready build over fastboot: slot b plus userdata, stock Android stays on slot a. Everything runs in this browser; nothing is uploaded.

Experimental: only continue if you know how to get back. Slot a still holds stock Android: fastboot set_active a then fastboot -w gets it back, or flash a stock ROM with the usual tools.

PHONE MUST BE IN FASTBOOT · BOOTLOADER UNLOCKED

Flash the postmarketOS server image

MI A3 / FASTBOOT

Writes vbmeta.img to vbmeta_b, erases dtbo_b, writes rootfs.img to userdata and boot.img to boot_b, then switches to slot b and reboots. Same steps as simple-install/flash.sh.

  1. 01

    Unlocked bootloader needed (fastboot flashing unlock, wipes the phone; see the README).

  2. 02

    Power off. Hold Volume Down + Power until the FASTBOOT screen shows, then plug in USB.

  3. 03

    Connect first: it tells you right away if the phone is busy. Then download the images or pick the three files you already have. Every file's size and SHA-256 is checked before anything is written.

Put the phone in fastboot mode, then connect.
